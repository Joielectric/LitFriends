Notes
  • Line breaks survive on HotAudio, except around links — so a blank line is put around every link line for you.
  • Borders sit on their own line. Some border characters are right-to-left and would scramble text sharing their line.
  • The opening line sits in a code block, which forces an even width. Links never go inside one — they stop working.
