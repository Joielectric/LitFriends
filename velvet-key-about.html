About

The Velvet Key

A showcase for intimate audio. A place for voice artists, writers and producers to give their work the home it deserves, and put it in front of the listeners who are looking for it.

What it is

The Velvet Key is a stage for independent creators. Each one gets a page that shows off their work, every release is gathered into one catalogue listeners can search by title and tag, and every name leads back to the person who made it. It is in a soft launch: open to listeners, and to creators by invitation.

For creators: show your work

  • Your own showcase page. Your work front and centre, with your story, links and latest news, in your own colours.
  • Seen by the right listeners. Every release in one catalogue, searchable by title, tag, audience and creator.
  • Searchable tags. Tag your work by audience and theme so the listeners looking for it find it.
  • Shared in a click. Turn any release into a ready-made Reddit, HotAudio or Literotica post, with a preview card that looks good wherever it’s linked.
  • Your back catalogue, brought in. Import what you’ve already posted on Literotica, ScriptBin, Soundgasm and HotAudio.
  • Your name on it. Voice, writing, production, music and art are each credited, and every name leads to its creator.
  • See it land. Listener feedback through your own form, and visitor numbers for your page.

For listeners: find it

  • One catalogue of everything. Audio, scripts, stories, poems and music from every artist, newest first.
  • Find what you’re in the mood for. Search by title, tag or creator, and filter by audience, type, platform or length.
  • Listen where you like. Each release links to wherever it’s posted, and some play right on the page.
  • Meet the artists. Every creator has a page with their work, links and latest news.
  • Say what moved you. Each creator has an anonymous feedback form, read by them and no one else.

How joining works
