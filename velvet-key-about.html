About

The Velvet Key

A home for voice artists, writers and producers to showcase their work. Every piece is crafted with intention, credited to everyone who made it, and made for adults who know exactly what they came for.

What it is

The Velvet Key gives independent creators one place to show their work. Every release lives in a catalogue listeners can search by title and tag, and every name leads to the person behind it. It is in a soft launch: open to listeners, and to creators by invitation.

For listeners

  • One catalogue of everything. Audio, scripts, stories, poems and music from every artist, newest first.
  • Find what you’re in the mood for. Search by title, tag or creator, and filter by audience, type, platform or length.
  • Listen where you like. Each release links to wherever it’s posted, and some play right on the page.
  • Meet the artists. Every creator has a page with their work, links and latest news.
  • Say what moved you. Each creator has an anonymous feedback form, read by them and no one else.

For creators

  • A page of your own. Your profile, links and news, in your own colours, with a preview card for when it’s shared.
  • Searchable tags. Tag your work by audience and theme so the right listeners find it.
  • Proper credit. Voice, writing, production, music and art are each credited, and every name links to its artist.
  • Bring in what you’ve made. Import your work from Literotica, ScriptBin, Soundgasm and HotAudio instead of typing it out.
  • Ready-made posts. Turn any release into a Reddit, HotAudio or Literotica post in a couple of clicks.
  • Hear from listeners. Your own feedback form, and visitor numbers for your page.

How joining works

For now, creators join by invitation. It keeps the collective small enough to know each other while it finds its feet.

  1. AskEmail joi@joielectric.com with a little about what you make and where we can hear it.
  2. Get your invitationIt goes to the Google account you’ll sign in with.
  3. Make it yoursSign in, build your page, and add your work, or import what you’ve already posted.
