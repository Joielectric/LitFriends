NaughtiWolf

Voice Artist · Dom · Collaborator

Commanding presence. Warm heart. The kind of voice that knows exactly what it wants — and exactly how to ask.

About

NaughtiWolf operates in the space where authority meets warmth — script fills, narrative collaborations, and his own creations. Stories built around MDom and soft MDom dynamics are his specialty, delivering a presence that's genuine even when it ventures into growling and roughness. He brings a storyteller's instinct to every piece, ensuring the power exchange feels earned, not just performative.

Whether he's stepping into a boyfriend experience, a MDom story with some bite, or building a narrative arc with a co-creator, his work carries the same through-line: honest feeling behind the control. He's a collaborator at heart — interested in what happens when two creative minds meet and push each other to something more fulfilling.
